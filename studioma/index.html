The Studio

From the first idea to the published post — on one board

Every new Studio starts with a ready-made workflow: Creation, Production and Publication. Rename it, add levels and columns, or start from scratch.

  • Smart handoff. When a script is approved, its card moves to Production by itself. It stays the same card: comments, files, history and assignees travel with it.
  • Cards that hold everything. Script, checklist, attachments, platforms, content type, due date, publication date and link.
  • Three views. Board, Calendar and My work — everything assigned to you across all your projects.
  • Approvals built in. Ask a client or a manager to approve; the card moves on when they say yes.
